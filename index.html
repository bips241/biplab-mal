About Me

Turning High-Concurrency Bottlenecks Into Resilient Systems

A focused backend engineer with production experience in microservices, event-driven pipelines, real-time protocols, and offline-first mobile databases.

I am a Backend & Distributed Systems Engineer dedicated to building fault-tolerant, high-throughput software architectures that thrive under heavy workloads.

My engineering focus centers on decoupling monolithic bottlenecks into lean, maintainable microservices and creating real-time collaborative applications. During my time at ScriptAssist in the United Kingdom, I decomposed a mission-critical healthcare pharmacy platform serving 25,000+ monthly active users into modular NestJS services, architecting inter-service TCP transport and Redis pub/sub streams to guarantee 99.9% system uptime.
